# M67 — Scan-backed source audit: 四部丛刊本《周易》卷一 v0.1

Status: source discovery advanced to an accessible page-image witness. Two folio images (DJVU pages 9 and 10) were opened and visually inspected for an initial spot-check. This is not a completed transcription verification, independent adjudication, or Gold admission.

## 1. Witness chain and bibliographic identity

The candidate is the first-series 四部丛刊 facsimile of 《周易》, volume 1–2, described by the CText library catalog as “景上海涵芬楼藏宋刊本” and as nine juan plus one juan of 略例. The attributed textual layers are listed as:

- 魏 王弼注
- 晋 韩康伯注
- 王弼撰《略例》
- 唐 邢璹注

Important distinction: the accessible Wikimedia file is a digital scan of the 四部丛刊 facsimile, not a newly photographed original Song woodblock copy. The underlying exemplar is catalogued as a Song printed witness held by 上海涵芬楼; the Four Books Congkan facsimile itself is a modern reproduction, with the first-series reprint recorded as 1922. Do not collapse the Song exemplar, 1922 facsimile, 2017 digital scan, and Wikisource transcription into one witness.

The Wikisource DJVU file page reports 2,591 × 4,217 pixels, 146 pages, 14.24 MB, with file upload dated 2017-05-07. The associated index lists the scan as not globally proofread/embedded; individual page entries can separately show “本页已校对”. That platform flag is useful metadata, not a substitute for project-level independent review.

## 3. Textual-layer finding: “夫易者象也” on page 9

The page 9 transcription places the following text inside the commentary/annotation brackets:

“夫易者象也象之所生生於義也有斯義然後明之以其物故以龍叙乾以馬明坤隨其事義而取象焉”

This passage must be attributed to the Wang Bi commentary layer in this witness, not automatically to the canonical 《文言》 or the received 《易传》 base text. On the same page, the surrounding base-text sequence includes:

“本乎天者親上本乎地者親下則各從其類也”

The page layout and transcription distinguish the commentary block from the base-text line. The distinction is methodologically important: the familiar received 《系辞下》 proposition “是故易者象也象也者像也” is a separate textual locus and must not be conflated with Wang Bi's commentarial wording on page 9.

The scan image was inspected at page 9. The relevant vertical text columns are visibly present, but this pass is only a visual spot-check. No character-by-character diplomatic transcription, variant collation, or independent adjudication has been completed.

## 4. Page 10 continuation

Page 10 transcription begins:

“自試也飛龍在天上治也亢龍有悔窮之災也乾元用九天下治也”

It then includes a bracketed Wang Bi commentary passage beginning:

“此一章全以人事明之也九陽也陽剛直之物也夫……”

The page 10 image was opened and visually compared at a preliminary level. The text layers and page continuity are useful for a future case, but no new claim is admitted from this page yet.

## 5. Provenance state and admission decision

Current project state for this source family:

- Source discovery: confirmed.
- Bibliographic description: catalogued as a facsimile of the Shanghai Hanfenlou-held Song printed witness.
- Digital scan: accessible; 146-page DJVU and page-level image derivatives identified.
- Page 9/10 visual spot-check: performed, not a complete transcription review.
- Transcription review: remains unreviewed for Gold purposes.
- Independent annotators/adjudication: not completed.
- Gold admission: **not admitted**.

The CText catalogue, Wikisource index, DJVU file page, page transcriptions, and page images are distinct provenance objects. The page-level “已校对” label on Wikisource does not satisfy M62's independent adjudication requirement. No source claim should be marked Gold until the project records exact spans, all referenced scan pages, two independent annotations, completed adjudication, accepted claim decisions, and an accessible scan URI.

## 6. Research consequence

This source candidate is now more actionable than a catalogue-only lead: page images are accessible, page locators are stable, and a commentary-layer passage about the generation and selection of images can be investigated against the facsimile. The next scholarly task is to collate this Wang Bi commentarial passage with earlier/later witnesses and with the separate 《系辞下》 base-text sentence, while retaining their distinct textual status. It is not yet evidence that the passage accurately describes the earliest historical formation of 易象 or that it validates a modern computational theory.
